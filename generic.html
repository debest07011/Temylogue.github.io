Temilogue

Hi, I’m Temi — the voice and pen behind Temilogue. I believe that writing is both mirror and window: a way to understand ourselves, and share what we see with the world.

I write essays, reflections, and poetic pieces that sit between thought and feeling. Sometimes I speak them aloud. Sometimes I let the words speak for themselves.

But always, I write to remember. To remember softness in a hardened world. To remember that silence has shape. That grief has color. That even the things we don’t say out loud deserve a place to rest.

Temilogue is not a blog. It’s not a podcast. It’s not a journal. It’s a quiet corner of the internet for the in-between. For the things we feel but can’t quite explain. For the stories we carry in our bones.

If you’ve ever felt like too much, or not enough, you’ll feel seen here. If you’ve ever looked at the sky and whispered “me too” to nothing — you’re not alone. If you write letters you’ll never send, or reread old messages just to feel something again — welcome.

This space is stitched together with vulnerability, honesty, and a little bit of magic. And I’m so glad you’re here.

Stay as long as you need. Return whenever your heart is heavy. There’s always a light on in Temilogue.
